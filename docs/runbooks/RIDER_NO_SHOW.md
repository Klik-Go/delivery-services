# Runbook: rider no-show

**When:** a rider accepted a job but doesn't arrive at the store (or went offline), or the
"no rider took it" alert fires.
**Who:** the zone coordinator.

1. **Console → Live orders** → the order: see the rider, their last location and when it updated.
2. Call the rider. If they will arrive within 5 minutes, add a note and wait.
3. Otherwise **Assign rider** and pick a free rider near the store (check them on the live map). The first rider loses the job; the new one gets an alert and the job in their app.
4. No free rider at all:
   - Call off-duty riders on the roster.
   - Tell the store (keep the food warm) and the customer (new time). Note both.
   - After 20 minutes with no rider, **Cancel** with reason "No rider available"; online
     payments are refunded automatically. Tell the customer.
5. The same rider twice in a week: talk to them; repeated no-shows → **Riders → Suspend** with the
   reason (BUSINESS §5.3).
6. Record the no-show in the order note; it is reviewed in the weekly KPI meeting (rider
   utilisation and completion rate).
